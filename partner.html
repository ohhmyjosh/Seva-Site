Seva / Partner

Let’s bridge the gap together.

We are looking for partners who can help move Seva from a working product to a credible local rollout — with real worker participation and real community trust behind it.

Ways in

Six tracks, each with a defined output.

Pick the one that matches what your organisation already does well. A narrow, well-matched partnership is worth more to us than a broad, vague one.

01

Field onboarding

Help register workers at labour areas — documents, explanation in Hindi, and the credibility of a familiar face. The highest-leverage help available today.

02

CSR support

Fund onboarding drives, QR posters, local awareness, and the operational cost of supervising a first rollout properly.

03

Community partnership

Collaborate as an organisation already close to the households and workers Seva serves — within the boundary that participation is never conditioned on faith.

04

Technical partnership

Realtime alerts, a payment integration, telephony credentials, analytics, reliability. The gaps between a working product and a pilot-ready one.

05

Local awareness

Build understanding of what Seva is for, through the neighbourhood channels that actually reach people.

06

Something else

If you can see a fit we have not listed, say so in the form. The model is deliberately not finished.

What you get back

Reporting that falls out of the data, not out of a slide deck.

Because verification is a database state and every job is OTP-confirmed against a specific booking, the questions a funder asks already have answers: who was onboarded, who was approved, which jobs completed, and what was earned.

We will also tell you plainly what is not working. A pilot that only reports its wins is not a pilot, it is marketing.

Worker outcomes
Onboarded, approved, active, repeat-booked — per worker, not only in aggregate.
Job records
Category, locality, duration and total for every completed booking.
Trust events
Approvals, rejections and suspensions, with the standard that was applied.
The four numbers
Bookings per day, worker retention, customer repeat rate, average transaction value.
Honest gaps
An explicit account of what did not work in the period, and what changed because of it.
